VAS · Peripheral & Venous

Circulation & vein conditions

Poor circulation, leg pain when walking, non-healing wounds, varicose veins, or problems with dialysis access.

Procedures
Peripheral angioplasty & stenting
Opens narrowed or blocked leg arteries to restore blood flow, often avoiding bypass surgery.
Varicose vein ablation
Closes off problem veins using heat or a sealant, done under local anaesthetic in under an hour.
Dialysis access intervention
Keeps the vein or graft used for dialysis working properly, avoiding delays to treatment.
What to expect: Day case, local anaesthetic in most cases, walking the same day.

ONC · Oncologic

Tumour-directed therapy

Liver, kidney, and other tumours — often for patients who aren't candidates for surgery, or as one part of a broader treatment plan.

Procedures
Chemoembolization (TACE)
Delivers chemotherapy directly to a tumour while cutting off its blood supply.
Y90 radioembolization
Delivers targeted radiation directly into a tumour, sparing healthy tissue.
Radiofrequency & microwave ablation
Destroys small tumours using heat, guided precisely by imaging.
What to expect: Usually a day case or one overnight stay; recovery is typically faster than surgery.

DX · Diagnostic & Access

Biopsy & access

The need for a tissue sample, a fluid collection that needs draining, or long-term access for medication or nutrition.

Procedures
Image-guided biopsy
Takes a tissue sample from precisely the right spot, with minimal discomfort.
Abscess & fluid drainage
Drains infected or built-up fluid without open surgery.
Central venous access (PICC / port)
Places a long-term line for chemotherapy, antibiotics, or nutrition.
What to expect: Local anaesthetic, typically done within the hour, home the same day.

MSK · Pain & Skeletal

Musculoskeletal

Chronic back pain from spinal fractures, worn or arthritic joints, and pain that hasn't responded to medication or physio.

Procedures
Vertebroplasty
Stabilizes a fractured spinal bone with medical cement, often relieving pain within days and avoiding the need for open spinal fusion surgery.
Genicular artery embolization
Reduces inflammation-driving blood flow to an arthritic knee, easing chronic pain without joint replacement.
Chronic pain management
Targeted, minimally invasive options for pain that hasn't responded to other treatment.
What to expect: Day case, local anaesthetic or light sedation, noticeable relief often within days to weeks.

MEN · Men's Health

Men's health

Varicoceles, enlarged prostate (BPH), and fertility-related concerns.

Procedures
Varicocele embolization
Closes off the problem vein through a small puncture, avoiding surgery.
Prostate artery embolization (BPH)
Shrinks the prostate by reducing its blood supply, easing urinary symptoms without surgery.
Male infertility-related interventions
Vascular treatment to support fertility, where appropriate.
What to expect: Day case, local anaesthetic, return to normal activity within a day or two.

WOM · Women's Health

Women's health

Uterine fibroids, pelvic congestion syndrome, and fertility-related blockages.

Procedures
Uterine fibroid embolization (UFE)
Shrinks fibroids by cutting off their blood supply, avoiding hysterectomy.
Fallopian tube recanalization
Clears a blocked fallopian tube to support fertility.
Pelvic congestion syndrome treatment
Closes off the problem veins causing chronic pelvic pain.
What to expect: Day case, local anaesthetic or light sedation, most return to normal activity within a few days.

LIV · Liver

Liver-directed care

Liver conditions requiring specialized vascular procedures, often alongside a broader treatment plan with a hepatologist or surgeon.

Procedures
TIPS
Creates a new pathway inside the liver to relieve pressure from advanced liver disease.
Portal vein embolization
Redirects blood flow to prepare the liver for surgery.
Liver biopsy
Takes a precise tissue sample using imaging guidance.
What to expect: Usually one overnight stay for monitoring.
